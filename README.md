My entry for make.fun's bounty "Promote make.fun's make-your-own-bounty post on X". The entry is a single quote-post on X: it quotes make.fun's announcement that anyone can now launch their own bounty, carries a photo of its own, and invites people to say what one-line bounty they would enter. Places are decided by the post's score — its replies plus its reposts plus its quotes — and the top 50 posts share $250.

The post: https://x.com/mhmmdzildan0312/status/2125056112072560785

`POST.json` names that post, and `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies it to the output directory, which is all the program has to do:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
